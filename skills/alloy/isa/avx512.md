# avx512 — AVX-512F (512-bit EVEX, opmask k0..k7)

Vector bytes: 64; lanes: f64: 8, f32: 16, f16: 32, i64: 8, i32: 16; opmask/predicate.

Legal schedules: scalar, scalar_remainder, masked_remainder, full_mask. gen_mask: _mm512_cmplt_epu{64,32}_mask(iota, set1(N - i)).

A scalar operand fuses into any arithmetic instruction as an EVEX embedded broadcast (`{1to8}`/`{1to16}`) when it comes from memory; a register scalar broadcasts explicitly.

The only x86 target with predication: masked tail forms take a leading __mmask8/16. avx512_256 (the matrix's -mprefer-vector-width=256) is this ISA at 256 bits: same intrinsics, half the lanes. Downclocking risk on some cores is why the width is a sweep knob. EVEX extras: embedded rounding, SAE, embedded broadcast {1to8}.

## Traps

- **select** (f64): operand-order trap: mask is FIRST and picks b - the opposite surface from AVX2 blendv; x86 calls this op blend
- **gather** (f64): operand-order trap: AVX-512 gathers take (index, base), AVX2 took (base, index)
- **select** (f32): operand-order trap: mask is FIRST and picks b - the opposite surface from AVX2 blendv; x86 calls this op blend
